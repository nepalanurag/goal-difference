Methodology

How the predictions are made, what goes in, how they score, and where they fall short.

The model

Each league is fitted separately. Two models run side by side and are blended into one headline probability:

  • Dixon-Coles Poisson. A per-league Poisson goal model with a time decay parameter (recent matches weigh more), an estimated home advantage, and a rho term adjusting for correlated low scores. Fitted by maximum likelihood on the finished fixtures.
  • Gradient boosting. Trained on the selected feature set from permutation-assisted selection (PAM), which keeps only feature groups that beat shuffled decoy copies. Config per league: Premier League: xgb_tiny, LaLiga: xgb_tiny, Bundesliga: xgb_reg, Serie A: xgb_tiny, Ligue 1: xgb_tiny.
  • Ensemble. The headline probability is 0.6 x ML plus 0.4 x Dixon-Coles. The weights are fixed, not tuned per matchday.

Inputs

  • Elo ratings
  • Market odds
  • Recent form
  • Venue splits
  • Attack / defense
  • Shot dominance
  • Discipline
  • Head-to-head edge
  • Rest and travel
  • European congestion
  • Key absences and injury drag

Team news: full injury data for the PL via FPL; other leagues use absences detected from lineups, labeled "reason unconfirmed". Forecasts freeze before kickoff; ledgers keep the misses.

Honest limitations

  • The market is a hard benchmark. In Premier League the de-vigged market odds beat the ensemble on walk-forward log-loss (0.9729 vs 0.9990). In LaLiga the de-vigged market odds beat the ensemble on walk-forward log-loss (0.9553 vs 0.9903). In Bundesliga the de-vigged market odds beat the ensemble on walk-forward log-loss (0.9666 vs 1.0083). In Serie A the de-vigged market odds beat the ensemble on walk-forward log-loss (0.9710 vs 1.0030). In Ligue 1 the de-vigged market odds beat the ensemble on walk-forward log-loss (0.9820 vs 1.0263). Odds compilers see team sheets, weather and late money; this model does not.
  • Feature selection is unstable. PAM selection run on two halves of the data agrees on a Jaccard similarity of 0.0 for every league. The selected set still beat the full set walk-forward for most leagues, but treat the "kept" groups as one draw, not the truth.
  • Probabilities are estimates. A 70% probability means the model expects that outcome 7 times in 10, not that it knows which. These numbers are not betting advice.
